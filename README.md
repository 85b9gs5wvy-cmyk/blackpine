Wilderness survival in the browser. Gather, build a claim, farm, crack keycard rooms, and hold your tool cupboard through the raid nights.

Press **Wake up (New Claim)**, pick a name, day length, gather rate, difficulty and starting loadout, or **Continue** your last claim. On a phone, turn it sideways.

## The basics

- **Start:** gather wood and stone, craft tools, and place a tool cupboard to hold your claim.
- **Crafting:** most crafting happens in the bag. Electrics, weapons, clothing and some parts need a workbench within about 8 m; guns, turrets and armour need a Tier 2 or Tier 3 bench. Some items [...]
- **Furnace:** slots 1–3 take Metal Ore or Sulfur plus Wood or Low Grade Fuel (fuel burns 3x longer). Ore becomes Metal Fragments, Sulfur becomes Smelted Sulphur, and burning wood leaves Charcoa[...]
- **Gunpowder:** 2 Smelted Sulphur + 2 Charcoal makes **5 Gunpowder**. No bench needed and the cost is fixed. Gunpowder goes into beancans, satchels and rockets.

## Farming

- Get seeds (hemp, corn, pumpkin) by harvesting the wild crops that grow along the rivers.
- Plant on open soil, or in a **Wooden Planter** (4 slots; you can stand on it).
- A **Water Jug** holds 5 uses. Fill it at a river or lake, then water your plants. Each watering lets a plant grow one stage (about 40 s of play per stage, whatever the day speed), and a plant ca[...]
- Harvest: pumpkins give 1–2 pumpkins and seeds, corn gives 2–3 cobs and seeds, hemp gives cloth, fiber and seeds.
- Your planted crops and planters are saved with the claim.

Green cards come from supply crates, blue from military crates, red from elite crates.

## Raid nights

- Every 5th day (days 5, 10, 15 and so on) is a raid day. At dusk you get a warning with the wave number. After dark the crew comes for your tool cupboard, or your bedroll if you have no cupboard.[...]
- Waves get bigger each time. Difficulty (Easy, Medium, Hard, Extreme) scales the size, toughness and gear. Your island rating (scavenge, hunt, build, blood) feeds the heat.
- On ordinary nights a few raiders still prowl. Raider camps guard loot. AI settlers claim a cupboard, build their own bases, arm up, and fight too.

## Cheats and low graphics

- Press ` or the **Cheats** button. The game pauses while the menu is open. Turn **Cheats: ON** to unlock God mode, Infinite needs, Peaceful, Free crafting, Fly, Super speed, item kits, teleports,[...]
- **Low graphics:** add `?low=1` to the URL, or toggle it in the cheat menu (cheats must be on). It lowers resolution, turns off shadows, and uses lighter models.
