## DONE: natural world overhaul (rivers, bridges, hills, biome trees, rocks, ground snow, undergrowth & wildlife)
- **Rolling hills & elevation strata**: `Hraw(x,z)` now adds domain-warped ridge chains and highland knolls (up to ~11.8 m, fading inside 85 m of spawn) on a 192×192 terrain mesh with rocky slope crags and alpine frost.
- **Meandering rivers**: `digRivers()` carves 3 winding river courses (`RIVERS`) around monuments, settler bases, ponds, and spawn, complete with an animated flowing water shader ribbon (`RWM`), sandy/mossy banks, river boulders, cattail reeds, and lilypads. Players can wade or drink from any river (`canDrink`).
- **Walkable bridges**: `buildBridges()` places 3D bridges (`BRIDGES`) in 3 styles (`truss`, `stone`, `foot`) perpendicular across rivers at road/trail crossings and wilderness crossings. `bridgeAt(x,z)` feeds `H(x,z)` for smooth ramp-to-deck elevation, and side railings use static post colliders. Rivers and bridges render on both the minimap (`MRD`) and full island map (`drawFull`).
- **Biome-specific tree species**: Added `firGeo` (subalpine fir on hills/pine forest), `oakGeo` (gnarled meadow oak), `mapleGeo` (scarlet sugar maple in autumn woods), `willowGeo` (weeping willow along rivers/lowlands), and `juniperGeo` (twisted desert juniper in dry scrub) alongside `pine`, `spruce`, `aut`, `aspen`, `birch`, `leaf`, `scrub`, and `snag`.
- **Diverse rocks & undergrowth**: Added `mossRockGeo`, `cragRockGeo`, `mesaRockGeo`, `fernGeo`, `flowerGeo`, `mushroomGeo`, mossy fallen logs with bracket fungi, soaring birds (`BIRDS`), and dusk fireflies (`fireflyPts`).
- **Dynamic ground snow**: `applySnowCover(snowAmt)` progressively blankets terrain vertex colors, tree canopies, rocks, grass, and roads in white snow when `wx === 'snow'`, reveals 3D ground snowdrifts (`snowDriftMesh`), kicks up snow footstep puffs, and melts away when weather warms.

## DONE: world generation bug fixes & 3D asset model smoothing / detail overhaul

- **Intersections, monument rings & snow cover**: Merged the spawn road elbow into a single smoothed path (`paveSpawnRoad`), flared/blended T-junctions in `buildRoadMesh`, prevented road posts (`POSTS`) from spawning on intersecting road lanes/bridges/rivers, cleared trees inside monument ring roads (`m.r + 12 m`), and added dynamic snow frosting to `ROADMAT` and `riverBankMesh` in `applySnowCover()`.
