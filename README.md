# Blackpine

Wilderness survival in the browser. Gather, build a claim, and hold the tool cupboard through every tenth night.

That button opens the game. Name a claim, then press **Wake up**. **Continue** loads the latest claim in this browser.

GitHub’s file list only shows source. The button above is what runs it. On a phone, turn it sideways.

## How to play

- **Computer.** WASD to move. Click the game, then the mouse looks. Shift sprints. E or click uses, gathers, and fires. R reloads. T swaps ammo. G drops the held stack (Shift+G drops one). V opens attachments. Z aims. F toggles the light. M opens the island map. On the map, scroll or pinch to zoom, drag to pan, and double-click or press the marker button to drop a pin. Pins save with the claim. 1–6 is the hotbar. I or Tab opens the bag. F5 quick-saves. F9 quick-loads. Esc pauses.
- **Phone.** Landscape only. Stick to move, drag to look, Use / Fire, Sprint, and Bag.
- **Claims.** On the title screen, set the name, day speed, gather rate, difficulty, and starting kit. Saved claims can be continued, renamed, cloned, exported, or deleted.

## Difficulty

Pick **Easy, Medium, Hard or Extreme** when you name a claim (or cycle it later in Pause > Settings). It is saved with the claim.

| | Easy | Medium | Hard | Extreme |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Raid size | 0.6x (max 24) | 1x (max 60) | 1.4x (max 64) | 2x (max 72) |
| Raider health / damage | 0.6x / 0.7x | 0.8x / 1x | 1x / 1.25x | 1.2x / 1.6x |
| Raider health growth per raid (max 1.8x) | +3% | +5% | +7% | +9% |
| Armor and heavy-build resistance (kept) | 50% | 65% | 80% | 100% |
| Raider gear tier | one raid behind | normal | one raid ahead | two raids ahead |
| Share of raiders sent at settlers | 2 in 10 | 3 in 10 | 4 in 10 | 5 in 10 |
| Night wanderers at once | 1 | 2 | 3 | 4 |
| Camp garrison | 0.7x | 1x | 1.25x | 1.5x (max 14) |
| Camp respawn | 4 min | 3 min | 2 min | 75 s |
| Camp sight range | 0.85x | 1x | 1.2x | 1.4x |
| Camp raiding parties | every 8 min, 2 raiders | every 5 min, 2 | every 3.3 min, 3 | every 2 min, 4 |
| Settler health | 1.3x | 1x | 0.9x | 0.75x |
| Settler guns | a raid early | normal | normal | a raid late |

**Settlers and camps.** Settlers without a gun (pistol or better) will not gather, wander or path inside a live camp's range. If they find themselves there they back out, and they run home from camp raiders. Once armed they stop avoiding camps. Raiders go for settlers too: camp towers snipe them, the garrison rallies on any settler it sees, and camps send raiding parties at the nearest settler on a timer. Wandering night raiders and siege raiders also pick settlers over you when the settler is closer.

## What’s new

- **Roads meet the bridges.** Rivers are cut before the roads are laid, so every crossing lines up with a bridge. The road runs straight onto the deck, both ends sit at the same height, and a short apron blends the road into the span. A road that only grazes a river is pushed onto the bank. No more stubs, and no bridges with nothing leading to them.
- **Smoother water and ground.** The terrain is finer. Riverbeds sit under the water so the bank no longer pokes through in a zigzag. A continuous bank and shoal follows each river, and the mouth flares where it meets the sea. Low meadows no longer puddle above the waves. Junctions blend, the road past where you wake is one smooth bend, and posts stay off intersections, bridges, and rivers. Trees are cleared inside the ring roads around monuments. Snow also frosts the roads and the riverbanks.
- **Sharper models.** Trees have root flares and limbs that join the trunk. Willows hang in long fronds. Rocks, ore, barrels, junk, machines, deer, wolves, the people, your hands, and the things you hold (berries, wood, meat, medkits, rations) are smooth instead of faceted.
- **Rivers, hills, and bridges.** The island rolls into ridges and highland knolls, up to about 12 m, and stays flatter near where you wake. Three rivers wind around monuments, settler bases, ponds, and your start. The water moves. Banks are sand and moss, with boulders, cattails, and lily pads. Stand in a river to drink. Roads and trails cross on bridges: steel truss, stone arch, or a narrow footbridge. Railings stop you. Rivers and bridges show on the minimap and the full map (**M**).
- **A thicker wild.** Hills grow subalpine fir. Meadows grow oak. Autumn woods grow scarlet maple. Willows hang over the rivers. Dry scrub grows twisted juniper, beside the pines, spruce, aspen, birch, and snags already on the island. Rocks come as mossy stones, crags, and mesa blocks. The ground has ferns, flowers, mushrooms, and fallen logs. Birds circle overhead. Fireflies come out at dusk.
- **Snow that sticks.** When the weather turns to snow, the ground, trees, rocks, grass, and roads go white, drifts appear, and your steps kick up snow. It melts when the weather warms.
- **Settler rigs and a greener island.** Every settler starts with a Pump Jack and a Quarry plus some Low Grade Fuel. They get stone, ore, sulfur, gears, circuits and fuel from those rigs, refuel them, and only chop trees for wood. If a rig is destroyed they go back to scavenging. Many more trees now grow across the whole island, including the dry and meadow areas.
- **Fight and tool fixes.** A melee swing now needs a clear line, so you cannot hit through a wall. Spears, knives, and clubs can break open barrels. Mounting a sight, barrel, or under-rail updates the gun in your hand, and those attachments save with the claim. If a gun breaks, the attachments come back to your bag. Extra rounds from a removed extended mag come back too. Bullets pass through bushes, hemp, and drops, and armor-piercing only punches through a living target. Stairs and camp ramps that run north–south can be walked. The revolver’s hammer sits under the sight so aiming down sights is clear. Hatchets and pickaxes are held in one hand. Tap the clock to change day speed: 1×, 3×, 5×, 10×, 15×, 20×, 30×, 60×, or 120×.
- **Bag and boxes.** The bag is a belt (keys 1–6) plus a 24-slot backpack. Click an item for a side panel: use it, split a stack, drop one or the whole stack, or sort the backpack. Crates, the storage box, the tool cupboard, the furnace, and the recycler open as real inventories. Drag stacks between them, or use Take all and Store bag. A crate stays empty until you loot it, then it waits out its timer and refills.
- **Worn gear.** The Wear tab shows a 3D figure. Drag to turn it. Helmet, vest, leg guards, and boots each cut damage, and the panel adds them up.
- **Map pins.** Press **M**. Scroll or pinch to zoom up to 10×, drag to pan, and double-click to drop a marker. Name it and change its color. Markers save with the claim. Roads, a north arrow, and a scale bar are drawn on the map. Camps stay red skulls with a headcount or a respawn timer.
- **Difficulty.** Easy, Medium, Hard, or Extreme, picked when you name a claim and changed later in Pause > Settings. The table is above.
- **Wood barricade.** Craft one from 30 wood. It is a 2.6 m plank wall (260 HP) that blocks movement and bullets. If a raider or a settler is shot with nothing between them and the shooter, they duck behind a barricade or set one down. Walls and doors stop bullets on a true line.
- **Raider camps.** Three occupied camps sit on the island. The **Raider Warlord Citadel** holds 8 to 10 raiders behind a skull gate, with watchtowers and a warlord. The **Raider Siege Armory** holds 6 to 8 around a munitions hangar and an armored truck. The **Raider Blood-Pit Stockade** holds 4 to 6 inside a timber palisade and a bonfire pit. They patrol until they see you, then the whole garrison is called in. Marksmen stay on the towers. They shoot, throw charges, and bandage. Wipe them and the game calls the **Raider War-Chests** open. Those chests hold guns, explosives, attachments, and scrap. Leave a few alive and they refill. Kill them all and a new crew retakes the camp in about 3 minutes and restocks the chests. On the map (**M**) camps are red skulls with a headcount, or a respawn timer once they are down.
- **Wild hemp.** Tall hemp plants grow across the island and a few stand by the road where you wake. Pick one for cloth and plant fiber. They grow back.
- **Barrel scrap.** Every barrel drops scrap, on top of whatever else is inside. Barrels are sealed: hold a rock, hatchet or pickaxe to break one open (hatchet and pickaxe wear down, the rock does not).
- **Solid world.** You, settlers, and animals stop on trees, rocks, ore, barrels, crates, and junk instead of walking through them. Props sit down into slopes so they don’t float, and they get pushed off the road.
- **Chop and mine feedback.** A swing kicks the camera. Wood chips, stone sparks, and a hit sound follow the tool.
- **Saved claims.** Name the claim before you wake. Pick day speed, gather rate, and a kit: Rock, Pioneer (tools and rations), or Outpost Builder (a cupboard and materials). **+ Starter Outpost** adds a fortified Day 3 camp. **F5** quick-saves. **F9** quick-loads.
- **Smarter fights.** Raiders strafe, flank, duck behind trees and rocks while they reload, and bandage when they are badly hurt. A shot calls nearby raiders in. Settlers do the same: they hold a gun line, heal, and repair broken walls, doors, and machines. They upgrade the camp door to sheet metal once they have the metal and a gear.
- **Deer and wolves.** Deer bolt as a herd when you get close, when a wolf is near, or when a gun goes off. Wolves hunt deer and call the pack. Both drop meat and leather.
- **Hands on the gun.** The viewmodel now has gloved hands. Two-handed tools and rifles use both.
- **Bigger island.** New monuments: satellite dish, oil depot, abandoned sawmill, power substation, and radar helipad, two of each, alongside the military outposts, warehouses, gas stations, and shacks. A road runs from where you wake toward the nearest settler and on to a monument. Trees now include spruce, aspen, birch, and scrub.
- **Doors upgrade.** Hit a door with the hammer to step it up, all the way to armored HQ metal.
- **3D items and drops.** Every item now has a 3D model in your hand and on the ground. Press **G** to toss the held stack (**Shift+G** for one). Dropped items arc, bounce, settle, hover and spin, merge with nearby stacks, and fly to you when picked up. The bag has Drop 1 / Drop Stack instead of Discard.
- **Tools gate gathering.** Bare hands can no longer chop trees or mine rocks. You wake with a **Rock** in your hotbar: it chops trees for 2 wood and chips stone for 1, and never breaks. Use it to collect 4 wood and 4 stone, craft a **Stone Hatchet** (needed for wood) and a **Stone Pickaxe** (needed for stone, ore and sulfur). Berries and loot still work by hand. Settlers ignore this rule. If you lose the Rock and lack both tools, you get a new one when you wake or load.
- **Scrap and research.** Scrap turns up in junk piles, barrels, crates and on raiders, and the recycler turns tech trash, springs, sheet, pipes and gun bodies into it. Build a **Research Table** (60 wood, 20 metal, 40 scrap), then spend scrap plus one copy of an item to learn its blueprint. 
- **Three workbench tiers.** Workbenches now cost scrap and come in Tiers 1 to 3 (the best one within 8 m counts):

  | Bench | Cost | Unlocks |
  |---|---|---|
  | Tier 1 | 40 wood, 12 stone, 6 metal, 20 scrap | components, clothing, melee, electrics, traps, Revolver |
  | Tier 2 (needs a Tier 1 in reach) | 60 wood, 40 metal, 2 gears, 60 scrap | pistol, shotguns, SMGs, gun bodies, turrets, targeting gear, special ammo, snap traps, HQ doors |
  | Tier 3 (needs a Tier 2 in reach) | 80 wood, 100 metal, 4 gears, 2 circuits, 150 scrap | rifles, AK, launcher, rockets, C4, explosives |

  Benches can research blueprints too, but at 2x the Research Table's scrap and only for items up to their own tier. Press Use on a bench to open crafting. Pipes, sheet metal, springs, gun bodies, guns, special ammo, rockets, C4, turrets and snap traps all start locked.
- Settlers craft weapons and get better ones as raid day nears: clubs and spears first, then knives and machetes, then pistols, shotguns, SMGs, rifles and AKs. Guns shoot raiders from range.
- Screen flashes red when you take damage. Crates hold 1 to 3 items. Roads route around settler bases.
- Full island map: press **M** (or tap the minimap) to open it, **M** or **Esc** to close. Shows monuments, bases, settlers, raiders, fires and you.
- Settlers now build in wood first, then upgrade their foundations, walls, roofs and stairs to stone, and to metal once they have metal.
- **Weapon attachments.** Holo sight, 8x scope, silencer, muzzle brake, muzzle boost, laser, flashlight and extended mag. Press **V** (or the Mods button, or Attachments on a gun in the bag) to mount them. Z or right-click aims down sights. F toggles the light and laser.
- Biomes: dark pine forest, autumn woods (orange and gold leaves, birch), open meadow, and dry scrub. Ground color, trees, rocks, bushes and grass all change with the biome, and the clock shows which one you are in. Forest patches have clearings.
- Compact vitals across the top. Weather and hunger alerts sit under them. Rank and heat stay on the left.
- Workbench components: rope, cloth, pipe, spring, sheet metal, gun bodies, tech trash, low grade fuel.
- New guns: pump shotgun, Thompson, semi-auto rifle. Armor, recycler, and medkits.
- Ammo types: standard, hollow point, armor-piercing, incendiary. Each gun remembers what is loaded.
- Settlers pull low grade fuel from red barrels so their furnace can finish.
